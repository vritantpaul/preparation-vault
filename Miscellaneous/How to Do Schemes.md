\>  What to Look for in a Scheme:

- **Objective:** The core purpose or goal of the scheme.
- **Launch Date:** When the scheme was officially launched.
- **Ministry:** The specific government ministry responsible for the scheme.
- **Entitlement:** What specific benefits or provisions are provided under the scheme.
- **Beneficiaries:** Identification of who is eligible to receive these benefits.
- **Updates:** Any recent changes, modifications, or status updates to the scheme.
