# Circular Flow of Income

START
Basic++
What are the five participants in the <b>circular flow of income</b>?
Back: 

1. Households
2. Firms
3. Financial Market*
4. Government
5. Foreign Sector

Footnotes: Four sectors and one intermediary—financial sector. Financial institutions are not treated as a separate sector because <u>they do not create new real output</u> in the circular flow of income.

## Financial Market

START
Basic++
What is the role of the <b>financial market</b> in the circular flow of income?
Back: 

1. To keep the savings of households, firms, and government.*
2. To provide borrowings to households, firms, and government.^


Footnotes: ^ Both for an interest, where, <b>interest earned on borrowings > interest paid for savings</b>. The difference is called <b>spread</b>.
Source: [[Circular Flow of Income (4 Sectors).jpg]]
