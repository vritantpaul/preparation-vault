What is the Heavily Indebted Poor Countries (HIPC) Initiative?
Back: A <u>joint Initiative by World Bank and IMF</u> to reduce external debt burden of poor countries by coordinated partial debt cancellation from multiple creditors, delivered in stages, conditional on reforms and poverty reduction policies.
Footnotes:
Source: Ch 23: International Economic Institutions (Indian Economy by Nitin Singhania)

Basic++
What is Multilateral Debt Relief Initiative (MDRI)?
Back: <u>Eliminates debt by providing full (100%) cancellation of eligible multilateral debt</u> from institutions like the IDA, IMF, and African Development Bank, for countries that have <u>successfully completed the HIPC process</u>.
Footnotes:
Source: Ch 23: International Economic Institutions (Indian Economy by Nitin Singhania)

START
Basic++
What is Crisis Response Window?
Back: Provides rapid, additional concessional financing from the IDA to help low-income countries respond to severe shocks—such as natural disasters, economic crises, or pandemics—<u>without reducing their regular IDA allocations</u>.
Footnotes:
Source: Ch 23: International Economic Institutions (Indian Economy by Nitin Singhania)

Basic++
What are the two main functions of Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA)?
Back: 
1. To promote FDI by providing insurance to private sector foreign investors against losses caused by non-commercial risks (political risks).
	Political risk includes:
	1. Currency transfer
	2. Expropriation*
	3. War and Civil Disturbance

2. To provide Credit Enhancement Guarantee to cross-border private sector investors and lenders.
