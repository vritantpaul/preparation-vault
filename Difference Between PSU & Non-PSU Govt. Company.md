|Dimension|PSU (Commercial Government Company)|Non-PSU Government Company|
|---|---|---|
|**Core Objective**|Commercial production, trade, banking, or infrastructure services to earn profits.|Public welfare, policy execution, skill building, or sector promotion.|
|**Legal Classification**|Government Company under Section 2(45) of the Companies Act, 2013 (or a statutory corporation).|Government Company under Section 2(45), often registered as a **Section 8 (not-for-profit)** company.|
|**Profit & Dividends**|Generates revenue from customers and pays annual dividends to the government.|Prohibited from paying dividends (if Section 8); any surplus is reinvested into its mission.|
|**Nodal Oversight**|Tracked and evaluated by the Department of Public Enterprises (DPE) as a CPSE.|Excluded from the DPE's commercial CPSE list; managed directly by its parent ministry.|
|**Funding Model**|Self-sustaining through commercial sales, fees, and market borrowing.|Funded primarily through government grants, CSR funds, or nominal user fees.|
|**Examples**|ONGC, NTPC, BHEL, IRCTC, SAIL|Digital India Corporation (DIC), Invest India, National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)|
