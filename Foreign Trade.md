What is the difference between CECA* and CEPA^?
Back: CECA involves <u>only tariff reduction or elimination on listed items</u> except the <b>negative list</b> and <b>Tariff Rate Quota (TRO)</b>$ items. Whereas, CEPA covers <u>trade in services and investments as well</u>. So CEPA is broader than CECA.
Footnotes: Comprehensive Economic Cooperation Agreement
^ Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement 
\***
$ A trade policy tool used in international trade that permits a certain quantity of a product to be imported at a lower tariff rate.
Source: Ch 22: India's Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade (Indian Economy by Nitin Singhania)

Basic++
One of the pillars of Foreign Trade Policy 2023 is the switch from incentive to remission. Explain.
Back: Instead of paying duties upfront and seeking rebates* later via scripts^\$, exporters receive remissions that allow them to bypass the initial payment burden, thereby streamlining the process and reducing administrative delays.
Footnotes: The exporter becomes eligible for rebate once the payment from the buyer is credited in foreign exchange. 
\***
^ Coupons
\*\*\*
$ This whole process is termed as "Duty Drawback (DBK)".
