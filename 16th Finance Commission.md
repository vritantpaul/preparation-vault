How is Per Capita GSDP Distance is calculated and used to determine horizontal devolution? 
Back: 
1. Identify top three states in Per Capita GSDP* (except Goa and Sikkim).
2. Take the average of the three as the benchmark.
3. The distance of the fourth highest Per Capita GSDP State from the benchmark, becomes the distance of the <u>first four highest Per Capita GSDP State</u>, along with Goa and Sikkim.
4. Calculate the gap of the rest of the states from the benchmark.
5. The lower the gap, the higher the devolution. The higher the gap, the lower the devolution.

Footnotes: $\text{Per Capita GSDP} = \frac{\text{Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP)}}{\text{Mid-year Population of the State}}$
Source: [[16th Finance Commission.pdf]]

Basic++  
What are the power-sector reforms recommended by the 16th Finance Commission to States?  
Back:  
1. Push for privatisation of DISCOMs.
2. Create SPVs to isolate debt of DISCOMs.
3. Use funds from SASCI to prepay or eventual repayment of debt.
4. Provide assistance only after privatisation process is complete. 
   
Footnotes: <u>Abbreviations</u>:
1. DISCOM: <b>Distribution Company</b> – A public or private utility responsible for distributing and supplying electricity to end-users. 
2. SPV: <b>Special Purpose Vehicle</b> – A distinct, legally separated entity created by a parent company to isolate financial risk, hold specific assets, or execute a particular project.
3. SASCI: <b>Scheme for Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment</b> – Provides 50-year interest-free loans to state governments for capital projects, aiming to boost capital expenditure and spur economic growth. 
   
Source: [[16th Finance Commission.pdf]]

What are the public-sector enterprise reforms recommended by the 16th Finance Commission to States?  
Back:  
1. Perform a review and closure of 308 inactive SPSEs*.

START  
Basic++  
What are the subsidy reforms recommended by the 16th Finance Commission to States?  
Back:  
1. Review and rationalise subsidy expenditure.
2. Avoid UCTs* as they tend to have large and untargeted beneficiaries.
3. Set clear exclusion criteria and rigorous review process to ensure effective targeting.
4. Discontinue financing of subsidies through off-budget borrowings.
5. Standardise what counts as a "subsidy" across different states.
6. Avoid misclassification of subsidies as assistance, grants, or other expenditure.
7. Adopt a uniform approach for accounting and disclosure of subsidies and transfers.
  
Footnotes:  Unconditional Cash Transfer
