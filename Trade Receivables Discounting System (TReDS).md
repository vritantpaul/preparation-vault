# How TReDS Works

1. **Factoring Unit (FU) Creation**: The MSME seller or the buyer uploads an invoice or bill of exchange to the platform, where it is converted into a standard digital instrument called a "Factoring Unit".

2. **Buyer Acceptance**: The buyer (corporate, government department, or PSU) digitally approves the invoice, confirming their obligation to pay on the due date.

3. **Competitive Bidding**: Multiple financiers (banks and NBFCs) bid to discount the invoice, offering various interest rates.

4. **Instant Disbursement**: The MSME selects the best bid and receives the funds—typically 80–90% of the invoice value—within 24 to 72 hours.

5. **Final Settlement**: On the maturity date, the buyer pays the full invoice amount directly to the financier.


## Hypothetical Example: "Agro-Tools Pvt Ltd"

1. The Transaction: Agro-Tools Pvt Ltd (an MSME) supplies tractor parts worth ₹1,00,000 to a large corporate, "Mega Motors". The credit period is 90 days, meaning Agro-Tools would normally have to wait three months to receive its payment.<br><br>

2. Creation of Factoring Unit (FU): Instead of waiting, Agro-Tools uploads the digital invoice to an RBI-approved TReDS platform like RXIL or M1xchange. This uploaded invoice is technically called a Factoring Unit (FU).<br><br>

3. Buyer Acceptance: Mega Motors logs into the same platform and digitally verifies that the parts were received and the invoice is legitimate. Once they "accept" the FU, it becomes a legally binding obligation for them to pay the amount on the 90th day.<br><br>

4. The Auction (Competitive Bidding): Multiple financiers (Banks or NBFCs) see this verified invoice. Because Mega Motors has a strong credit rating, they bid competitively to buy the invoice.
    
    - Bank A bids with a discount rate of 8% p.a.
        
    - Bank B bids with a discount rate of 7.5% p.a.<br><br>

5. Instant Disbursement: Agro-Tools selects Bank B's better offer. The financier deducts the 90-day interest (roughly ₹1,850 in this case) and transfers approximately ₹98,150 to Agro-Tools’ bank account within 24–48 hours.<br><br>

6. Final Settlement: 90 days later, the TReDS platform automatically triggers a payment through the National Automated Clearing House (NACH). ₹1,00,000 is debited from Mega Motors' account and credited to Bank B.
