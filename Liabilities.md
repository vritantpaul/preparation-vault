> [!IMPORTANT] Definition
> Liabilities are probable future sacrifices of economic benefits arising from present obligations of a particular entity to transfer assets or provide services to other entities in the future as a result of past transactions.

##### Breakdown
1. **Probable**
	Liabilities involve uncertainty and judgment in estimating future outcomes, especially with accruals.<br><br>
2. **Future sacrifices**
	Future sacrifices refer to giving up economic benefits such as assets and services, not limited to cash.<br><br>
3. **Present obligations**
	- Liabilities are present obligations to transfer assets or provide services in the future.
	- Sellers sometimes offer credit terms to incentivize buyers to spend more money and bring their purchases forward.
	- Credit terms provide flexibility in managing cash flow, with payment typically due within 30 days of receiving the invoice.<br><br>
##### Why are liabilities Important?
Liabilities are essential for business financing and operations. Liabilities are *a source of third-party funding* used to buy assets and fund operations.

##### Types of Liabilities

1. **Current Liabilities**: They are business' obligations that need to be settled within one year from now.
	- Most common type of current liability is accounts payable. *Accounts Payable* is recognized when the buyer receives an invoice from the supplier, representing the amount owed.
	- Other types of current liabilities include
		- *Salaries Payable*
		- *Taxes Payable*
		- *Interest Payable* 
		- *Unearned Revenue* (Postpayments)
		- *Short-term Loans*
		- *Accruals* (Accrued Expenses)
			- Accruals help recognize expenses that have been incurred but not yet recorded in the books.
			- Accruals ensure that the business's income statement and [[Balance Sheet|balance sheet]] accurately reflect the financial performance.<br><br>
2. **Non-current Liabilities**: Obligations that aren't expected to be settled within a year. 
	- Non-current liabilities include
		- *Long-term Loans*
		- *Bonds*
		- *Mortgages*
		- *Pensions*
		- *Deferred Income Tax*<br><br>
3. **Contingent Liabilities**: A contingent liability is a potential obligation that may arise depending on the outcome of an uncertain future event.
