What are the features of the <b>PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)</b> scheme?
Back: 
1. Refinances agencies i.e., <u>does not provide loans directly to the loanee</u>, instead finances Last Mile Financers*.
2. Loan proposal <u>should be for setting up of a new/upgrading</u> an existing Micro business enterprises.
3. Loans available for purchase of Transport Vehicle / Tractors, tillers as well as two wheelers, in case <u>applicant intends to use vehicle for commercial purposes</u>.
4. Applicant <u>should not be defaulter of any Bank</u>/Financial institution to get benefit.
5. [[Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro & Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)]] created to mitigate issue of collateral.
6. Life insurance not mandatory for loans under PMMY.
7. PAN not compulsory for loan to avail.
   
Footnotes: Such as Non-Banking Finance Companies, Micro Finance Institutions, Societies, Trusts, Section 8 Companies, Small Finance Banks and Regional Rural Banks.
Source: [[PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY).pdf]]

What are the different types of loans under <b>PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY)</b> scheme?
Back: 
1. <b>Shishu Loan:</b> up to Rs 50,000
2. <b>Kishore Loan:</b> above Rs 50,000 and up to Rs 5 lakh
3. <b>Tarun Loan:</b> above Rs 5 lakh and up to Rs 10 lakh
4. <b>Tarun Plus Loan:</b> above Rs 10 lakh and up to Rs 20 lakh*

Footnotes: 20 lakh loan amount applicable for those who have successfully repaid previous loans under the 'Tarun' category.
