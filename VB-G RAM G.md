What are the features of the <b>VB-G RAM G</b> scheme?
Back: 
1. Statutory guarantee of 125 days* of wage employment per rural household per year.
2. Weekly wages or within 15 days of work.
3. Unemployment allowance if work not provided within 15 days of demand.
4. Up to 60 "no-work days" during peak sowing and harvesting.
   
Footnotes: From 100 days in MG NREGA.
Source: [[VB-G RAM G.pdf]]
