# Promotion of FDI through Political Risk Insurance and Credit Enhancement

## 1. Promotion of FDI through Political Risk Insurance

Institutions like the Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA), a part of the World Bank Group, promote Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) by providing insurance to private foreign investors against **non-commercial risks (political risks)**.

### Why is this needed?
Foreign investors are often reluctant to invest in developing or politically unstable countries due to risks arising from:
- Government actions
- Political instability
- Policy uncertainty

These risks are not related to business performance, hence called **non-commercial risks**.

### Objective:
To reduce investor uncertainty by assuring:
> Compensation in case political risks lead to losses.

---

## 2. Types of Political Risks Covered

### (i) Currency Transfer Risk

#### Meaning:
Risk that an investor cannot:
- Convert local currency into foreign currency, or  
- Transfer profits, dividends, or loan repayments out of the host country  

#### Causes:
- Foreign exchange controls  
- Capital restrictions imposed by the government  
- Economic or balance of payments crises  

#### Impact:
- Profits remain **blocked in the host country**
- Investor cannot repatriate earnings  

#### Insurance Coverage:
- Compensation for inability to convert currency  
- Protection against transfer delays or restrictions  

---

### (ii) Expropriation

#### Meaning:
Government actions that **deprive investors of ownership, control, or value of their investment**.

#### Types:

1. **Direct Expropriation**
   - Nationalization or seizure of assets  
   - Example: Government takes over a foreign-owned company  

2. **Indirect (Creeping) Expropriation**
   - Gradual measures reducing investment value  
   - Example:
     - Excessive taxation  
     - Cancellation of licenses  
     - Restrictive regulations  

#### Impact:
- Loss of ownership or control  
- Reduction in economic benefits  

#### Insurance Coverage:
- Compensation for loss of investment  
- Protection against government interference  

---

### (iii) War and Civil Disturbance

#### Meaning:
Losses arising from political instability or violent events.

#### Includes:
- War (internal or external)  
- Civil war  
- Revolution  
- Riots, terrorism, insurgency  

#### Impact:
- Physical destruction of assets  
- Disruption of business operations  
- Loss of income  

#### Insurance Coverage:
- Compensation for physical damage  
- Coverage for business interruption losses  

---

## 3. Credit Enhancement Guarantee

### Meaning:
Provision of guarantees to **cross-border private sector investors and lenders** to reduce the risk of default.

### How it works:
- A project seeks funding in a foreign country  
- Lenders perceive high risk  
- A guarantee agency promises:
  > To repay lenders if the borrower defaults  

### Effects:
- Reduces risk for lenders  
- Improves creditworthiness of projects  
- Encourages lending and investment  
- Lowers cost of borrowing  

### Example:
A power project in a developing country:
- Without guarantee → difficult to obtain loans  
- With guarantee → lenders feel secure → financing becomes available  

---

## Final Summary

- **Political Risk Insurance** → Protects investors from losses after investment  
- **Credit Enhancement Guarantee** → Helps investors secure funding before investment  

### Overall Impact:
- Reduces uncertainty  
- Increases investor confidence  
- Promotes Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in developing and high-risk countries
